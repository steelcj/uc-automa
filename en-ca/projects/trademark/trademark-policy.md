# Universal Cake trademark policy

You may use Universal Cake code and content freely under their licences, but only the official project may use the Universal Cake name and logo to identify a product, service, evaluation or radar.

Status: provisional, pending confirmation by the maintainer.

## Why this policy exists

Universal Cake code is licensed under MIT and its content under CC BY 4.0, so anyone can copy, change and republish it. Neither licence covers the name or logo. This policy keeps the name meaningful: when people see "Universal Cake", they should know the work came from the project and was scored against the published Evaluation Metrics.

## What the marks are

- The name "Universal Cake", and close variants such as "UniversalCake" and "UC Radar".
- The Universal Cake logo and icons.
- Any other name or design the project identifies as a mark on its official site.

## Uses that need no permission

- Saying that your work is based on, uses or is compatible with Universal Cake, for example "built on Universal Cake tools" or "scored with the Universal Cake Evaluation Metrics, version 2.0".
- Redistributing unmodified official releases under their original names.
- Writing about, reviewing, teaching or criticizing Universal Cake.
- Linking to the official project with its name or logo.
- Quoting an official radar evaluation unchanged, with credit and a link.

## Uses that need permission

- Naming a fork, product, service or company "Universal Cake" or anything confusingly similar.
- Using the logo on modified tools, a hosted service or merchandise.
- Presenting a modified or independent evaluation as an official Universal Cake evaluation or placement on the official radar.
- Implying that the project or Vishpala endorses, certifies or partners with you.
- Using the marks in domain names, social media handles or app names.

## If you modify the work

- Give your version its own name.
- Say what you changed and which official version you started from, as CC BY 4.0 already requires for content.
- Keep the original copyright and licence notices, as MIT requires for code.
- Make clear that your version is not the official project.

## Official sources

Official Universal Cake releases and evaluations are published at [github.com/steelcj](https://github.com/steelcj). Anything published elsewhere under the name should link back there.

## Asking for permission

Requests to use the marks in ways not covered above can be sent to the maintainer through the official repository. The project intends to say yes to uses that help people, especially non-profit, educational and accessibility work, as long as the use is not confusing.

## Licence of this policy

This policy covers trademarks only. It does not change the MIT or CC BY 4.0 licences on Universal Cake code and content, and those licences do not grant trademark rights.

This policy is not legal advice. It should be reviewed by a lawyer familiar with Canadian trademark law before it is relied on.
