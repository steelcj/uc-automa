# Decision: Session Records

Version: 0.1.0
Status: Accepted

## Context

Design work happens in sessions whose reasoning is lost once the session ends. Balise already keeps such records. The decision was worked out with Christopher Steel on 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-09 and recorded in the foundation brief <a id="cite-steel-2026b"></a>[(Steel, 2026b)](#ref-steel-2026b).

## Decision

Every repository may keep dated session records at `{language}/docs/sessions/{date}--{slug}{version}.md`: what was decided, what was made, and what was left open.

## Consequences

- The base layout describes `sessions/` as optional and closed.
- The foundation build's own record is the first, at `en-ca/docs/sessions/2026-10-09--uc-automa-foundation-v0.1.0.md`.
